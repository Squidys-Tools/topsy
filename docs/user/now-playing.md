# Now Playing

A tray opened to look (a click on the notch, the tray icon) opens on its Home page, which leads with a player for
what Windows is playing:
the session the volume flyout shows, such as Spotify, a browser tab or the Media Player. It has the art, title
and artist with level bars beside them, the elapsed time, a progress bar and the time left, then previous,
play/pause and next. A button the app doesn't take is greyed. The [shelf](shelf.md) is the next page (see
[The notch](notch.md)).

While something is playing (not paused), the peek shows its art, title and level bars, with the shelf
count beside it. Stopped, or no session, shows nothing. A tray opened by dragging up to the edge or by a drop shows only the shelf.

## Floating player

Drag the player out of the open notch and it floats wherever you let go, a small glass window of its own that
stays on top of other windows without ever taking the focus. The button at the right of the player's buttons
does the same (click it again to put the player away), and so does *Show floating player* in the
[tray icon's menu](settings.md).

It comes in three sizes: **Mini** (the art, the title and play/pause), **Player** (everything the notch's player
has) and **Expanded** (a large cover over the player). The buttons beside play/pause grow and shrink it; the
cross puts it away. Drag it by anything but its buttons to move it. Swipe sideways on a touchpad over it to skip
to the next track, or back to the previous one. It comes back where you left it, at the same size, after a
restart.

With nothing playing it stays where it is and says so, ready for the next song. Its level bars move only while
the pointer is over it, so a player left on screen costs next to nothing.

## Good to know

- The level bars follow the default output device, so they move with everything playing, not only the session
  shown. A player routed to another output (per-app output in Sound settings) shows resting bars.
- The player shows the app Windows picks, as the volume flyout does. When another app is playing or paused
  too, the player names the app beside its buttons: click the name to switch to the next one. topsy stays with
  your pick until that app closes its session, or until another app starts playing while yours is paused.
- A long title scrolls slowly side to side in the notch's player; the peek and the floating player cut it short.
- The floating player has no lyrics or *Playing Next* queue, as Droppy's does: Windows doesn't tell other apps
  either.
